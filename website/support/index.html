Support

Destek

Simple help for a simple-looking but deep-working oral care product.

Sade gorunen ama derin calisan agiz bakimi urunu icin yalın destek sayfasi.

FAQ

  • What is Dentli?
    Dentli is a personal oral care tracking app.
  • Does Dentli provide medical advice?
    No. It does not diagnose, treat, or replace professional care.
  • Do I need an account?
    No mandatory account is required in the current build.
  • Where is my data stored?
    Most data is designed to stay on your device.
  • How do reminders work?
    Dentli uses optional local notifications based on your settings and entries.
  • How do I delete data?
    Delete records in the app where supported, clear app data, or uninstall the app.
  • How does Pro work?
    Dentli Pro is intended as a one-time unlock.

Basics

Use Today for fast daily actions, Map for tooth tracking, Timeline for history, Appointments for visits, and Care for replacement cycles.

Troubleshooting

  • Restart the app if a screen looks stale
  • Check notification permissions if reminders are not arriving
  • Use native builds for best local-data behavior
  • Contact support for purchase restore or data concerns

Sik Sorulan Sorular

  • Dentli nedir?
    Dentli kisisel agiz bakimi takip uygulamasidir.
  • Dentli tibbi tavsiye verir mi?
    Hayir. Teshis koymaz, tedavi sunmaz ve profesyonel bakimin yerine gecmez.
  • Hesap gerekli mi?
    Mevcut surumde zorunlu hesap yoktur.
  • Verilerim nerede saklanir?
    Verilerin buyuk cogu cihazinizda kalacak sekilde tasarlanmistir.
  • Hatirlaticilar nasil calisir?
    Dentli, ayarlariniza ve kayitlariniza gore istege bagli yerel bildirimler kullanir.
  • Verilerimi nasil silerim?
    Desteklenen alanlarda kayitlari silin, uygulama verisini temizleyin veya uygulamayi kaldirin.
  • Pro nasil calisir?
    Dentli Pro tek seferlik kilit acma modeli icin tasarlanmistir.
