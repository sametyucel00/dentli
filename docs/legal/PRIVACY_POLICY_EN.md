# Dentli Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 5, 2026

## 1. Overview

Dentli is a personal oral care tracking app. Dentli is designed to help you record routines, symptoms, appointments, care-item replacement cycles, and tooth history.

Dentli is a non-diagnostic product. It is not a medical service, does not provide medical advice, and does not create a patient-provider relationship.

## 2. Who this policy applies to

This Privacy Policy applies to the Dentli mobile app and the Dentli support and legal pages.

Contact:

- Dentli support email: sametyucel52@gmail.com

## 3. What Dentli stores

Dentli is a local-first app. Most information is stored on your device, not on Dentli-operated servers.

Depending on how you use the app, Dentli may store the following on your device:

- profile names you create in the app
- preferred language and theme settings
- oral care routine settings
- hygiene event history
- symptom logs you enter
- appointments you create
- care item and replacement tracking data
- tooth status and tooth history entries
- app entitlement status for the Pro unlock in the current build

Some of this information may be health-adjacent or sensitive because it relates to oral care and symptoms. Dentli is designed to minimize collection and keep this information local where possible.

## 4. What Dentli does not collect by default

Dentli does not require account creation.

Dentli does not intentionally collect or operate its own cloud account database for your routine entries in the current build.

Dentli does not use advertising SDKs.

Dentli does not sell your personal data.

Dentli does not ask for contacts, camera, microphone, or location permissions in the current build.

## 5. Notifications

If you allow notifications, Dentli may schedule local reminders on your device for:

- morning or night brushing
- floss
- mouthwash
- toothbrush replacement
- dental check reminders
- appointment reminders

These reminders are generated from your local app settings and entries. Notification permissions are optional and can be changed in your device settings.

## 6. Purchases

If you buy Dentli Pro, the payment transaction itself is handled by Apple or Google through their store systems.

Dentli does not receive your full payment card details.

Dentli may store limited purchase-related status needed to unlock Pro features, restore access, and show your entitlement state inside the app.

## 7. Third-party services

Dentli relies on mobile platform services and app infrastructure tools, which may include:

- Apple App Store / StoreKit for iOS purchases
- Google Play / Play Billing for Android purchases
- Apple and Google notification systems
- Expo platform libraries used to run the app

In the current build, Dentli is designed to avoid unnecessary third-party data sharing beyond what is needed for app distribution, billing, and platform functionality.

## 8. Purpose limitation and data minimization

Dentli aims to follow privacy-by-design principles, including:

- collecting as little data as practical
- using data only for the app features the user expects
- keeping routine and oral care records local where possible
- avoiding advertising and unrelated profiling

## 9. Legal basis style summary

Where privacy laws such as GDPR or KVKK apply, Dentli generally relies on:

- your request to use the app’s features
- your choice to enter data into the app
- your choice to enable notifications
- compliance with basic legal obligations where required
- legitimate interest in providing, securing, and supporting the app in a limited and proportionate way

## 10. Data deletion and user control

Because Dentli is local-first, you generally control your data directly on your device.

You can remove data by:

- deleting records inside the app where supported
- removing profiles inside the app where supported
- uninstalling the app and clearing app data through your device settings

## 11. Retention

In the current local-first model, data remains on your device until you edit or delete it, clear app data, or uninstall the app.

Purchase-related and platform-level records may remain with Apple or Google under their own policies.

## 12. Children

Dentli is not directed to children under the age required by applicable law to consent independently to data processing.

## 13. Security

Dentli aims to reduce risk by storing most app data locally rather than operating a central cloud database in the current build.

No app can guarantee absolute security. You are responsible for controlling access to your device, backups, and platform account.

## 14. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights such as:

- asking what data is processed
- asking for correction or deletion
- objecting to certain processing
- withdrawing consent where consent is used
- lodging a complaint with a relevant data protection authority

Because Dentli is primarily local-first, many of these rights are exercised directly by controlling the data on your own device.

Dentli may update this Privacy Policy if app features, legal requirements, or platform integrations change.
