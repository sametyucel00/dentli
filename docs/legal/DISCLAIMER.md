# Dentli Disclaimer

## Short disclaimer

Dentli is a personal oral care tracking tool. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

## Long disclaimer

Dentli helps users track routines, symptoms, appointments, care items, and oral care history. Dentli does not diagnose conditions, recommend treatment, or replace a dentist, doctor, or emergency service. Any insights, reminders, summaries, or exports are based on user-entered information and product logic. If you have pain, swelling, bleeding, trauma, infection concerns, or any urgent health issue, contact a qualified healthcare professional.
